# Minors and Double Counting

IIT Madras offers approved Minors in various fields such as:
- Data Science
- Design
- Mathematics
- and others

Students can complete additional elective requirements to earn a Minor alongside their degree.

## Minor Requirements

To earn a Minor, students must complete:

```text
4 Courses
```

from the approved course basket of that Minor.

This usually corresponds to:

```text
Minimum ~36 Credits
```

The courses used for the Minor must be elective courses and not part of the student's core curriculum.

## Number of Minors Allowed

Students are allowed to complete:

```text
Maximum 2 Minors
```

during their degree.

## Double Counting Rules

Double counting is generally not allowed.

This means:
- A course counted toward one Minor cannot count toward another Minor.
- Courses from the parent department generally cannot be used to satisfy Minor requirements.

Example:
- If a student pursues both Aerospace and Physics Minors, separate courses must be completed for each.

## Important Clarification

Minor courses can be completed within the normal degree credit requirements.

However:

> Honours credits must be additional to the regular graduation credits.

This means:
- Minor requirements may fit within normal degree credits
- Honours requires additional credits beyond the standard degree requirement

## Claiming Minors

Before graduation:
- forms are circulated by the Academic Section
- students must declare completed Minor requirements through those forms

To claim a Minor:
- minimum 36 credits from the approved basket must be completed

## Official Lists

Students should check the latest approved lists through:
- [Academic Portal](http://Academic.iitm.ac.in)
- Course Documents section

because course baskets and approved lists may change over time.

One of the most common IITM experiences is:
```text
"I accidentally completed half a minor."
```

Usually discovered during 3 AM degree audits before registration.
