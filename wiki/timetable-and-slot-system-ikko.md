# Timetable and Slot System

> Understanding slots early saves you from discovering in registration week that your dream elective and your mandatory core course are fighting to the death in the same timetable cell.

IIT Madras follows a fixed slot-based timetable system. Every course is assigned a predefined slot such as A–H, J–M, or P–S. These slots determine when classes occur during the week and are designed to prevent timetable clashes between courses.

Official timetable reference:  
[Time Table IITM](https://www.iitm.ac.in/academics/academic-services/timetable)

Useful timetable generator:  
(Time Table Generator)[https://tt.mathiitm.com/]

---

## How Slots Work

- Most lecture slots are fixed across the institute.
- Courses in the same slot cannot be taken together.
- Slots A–J are generally 4-hour slots.
- Slots K–M are usually 3-hour slots.
- First-year students may additionally have slots P–S.

### Example

- A course in Slot A always occurs during the same scheduled timings every week.
- If two electives share Slot C, you must choose one.

---

## Why Slots Matter

Understanding the slot system becomes extremely important during:

- Course registration
- Elective planning
- Minor planning
- Workload balancing
- Internship semester optimization

---

## Tips for Freshers

### Use a Timetable Generator

Before finalizing electives, visualize your week using timetable tools.

### Check Lab Timings Carefully

Some labs consume large uninterrupted blocks.

### Avoid Overloading Difficult Slots

Having multiple heavy courses back-to-back becomes painful during quiz season.

### Ask Seniors

Course difficulty and workload vary heavily depending on the instructor.
