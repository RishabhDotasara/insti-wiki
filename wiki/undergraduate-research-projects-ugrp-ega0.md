# Undergraduate Research Projects (UGRP)

Apart from regular coursework and projects, students can also take Undergraduate Research Projects (UGRP) to gain research experience during their degree.

UGRPs allow students to work under faculty supervision on research-oriented problems and projects.

## Credit Structure

Each UGRP is generally worth:

```text
9 Credits
```

Students are allowed to take up to:

```text
18 Credits
```

of UGRP that can be counted within the allowed elective structure.

## Course Code

The commonly used UGRP course code is:

```text
ID4900
```

## Credit Counting Rules

If the research project is supervised by faculty from:
- Parent department
- Approved sister departments

then the credits may count toward Professional Electives.

Otherwise, they are usually counted as:
- Unallotted Credits

The final category allocation is subject to Academic Section approval.

## Registration Process

To register a UGRP:
1. Obtain faculty approval
2. Mail the Academic Section with approval details
3. Add the course through workflow

## Why Students Take UGRP

Students generally pursue UGRP for:
- Research exposure
- Higher studies preparation
- Exploring specialized fields
- Working closely with professors and research groups

UGRP also gives students the full authentic research experience:
- reading papers
- debugging things that should theoretically work
- questioning life choices at 2 AM in a lab

## Important Notes

- UGRP is optional.
- UGRP credits are separate from regular project courses like BTP/DDP.
- The allowed number of research credits is capped.

Students should confirm credit allocation rules with:
- Faculty advisor
- Department office
- Academic Section

before registration.
