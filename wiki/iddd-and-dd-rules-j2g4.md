# IDDD and Dual Degree Rules

Students at IIT Madras may apply for:
- Inter-Disciplinary Dual Degree (IDDD)
- Dual Degree (DD)

subject to institute eligibility requirements.

## Withdrawal

Students are allowed to withdraw from the program at any stage, including:
- 8th semester
- 10th semester

subject to institute procedures.


>[!IMPORTANT]
> This is not possible now after the revised rules, ask your seniors to confirm.


## Eligibility for IDDD/DD Upgrade

As per the updated Senate policy:

By the end of the 5th semester, students must have:
- CGPA of 8.0 or greater
- Minimum 250 successfully completed credits

to become eligible to apply.

Backlogs no longer prevent application provided:
- minimum credit requirements are satisfied
- CGPA requirements are satisfied on time

## Applying in Later Semesters

If applying for conversion at a later semester:
- students must complete at least an additional 50 credits per semester
- maintain a CGPA of 8.0 or greater

The conversion will only be considered if:
- the remaining curriculum requirements for DD can still be completed from that point onward

## Important Notes

- Eligibility depends on both CGPA and completed credits.
- Meeting eligibility does not automatically guarantee conversion.
- Curriculum feasibility is also checked during approval.

At IITM, even upgrading your degree comes with prerequisite checks.

The institute truly believes in consistency.
