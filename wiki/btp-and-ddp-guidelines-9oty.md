# BTP and DDP Guidelines

Final-year project courses at IIT Madras are generally offered in the form of:
- B.Tech Project (BTP)
- Dual Degree Project (DDP)

These projects allow students to work on larger academic, research, or application-oriented problems under faculty supervision.

## B.Tech Project (BTP)

The B.Tech Project (BTP) is a final-year project course.

Typical features:
- Usually taken during the final year
- Carries significant credit weight
- Optional for regular B.Tech students
- Mandatory for students pursuing Honours

Students work under a faculty guide and complete project-related work across the semester.

## Dual Degree Project (DDP)

Students enrolled in Dual Degree programs complete a Dual Degree Project (DDP).

The DDP is similar in structure to the BTP but is mandatory for Dual Degree students.

It forms an important component of the Dual Degree curriculum.

## Registration Process

BTP/DDP registration is generally done through workflow.

After registration:
- The course coordinator may circulate forms to collect project details
- Faculty guides submit grades to the course coordinator
- Grades are then uploaded to workflow

If no faculty guide is assigned, students may receive:
```text
I Grade
```

which indicates an incomplete status.

## Common Course Codes

Typical course codes include:

### Department Project
```text
ME4490
```

### Interdisciplinary/Other Department Project
```text
ID4951
```

The category under which the project is counted may depend on:
- Department
- Faculty guide
- Curriculum rules

## Important Notes

- BTP/DDP credits are usually large compared to regular electives.
- Students should confirm project registration deadlines carefully.
- Faculty approval is required before project allocation.

Most students enter BTP imagining groundbreaking innovation.

Most students leave with:
- a report
- several sleepless nights
- and a deep emotional connection to LaTeX compilation errors
