# Honours Degree

The Honours program allows students to pursue additional coursework beyond the regular B.Tech curriculum.

Students graduating through this track receive an Honours degree mention along with their regular degree.

## Credit Requirement

Students pursuing Honours are required to complete additional credits on top of their regular degree requirements.

Typically, this involves:

```text
Regular Degree Credits + Additional 27 Credits
```

These additional credits are generally completed during the later semesters.

## Eligibility Criteria

To apply for Honours, students are generally required to:
- Maintain a high CGPA
- Have no active backlogs or disallowed grades
- Continue maintaining the required CGPA throughout the remaining semesters

The exact eligibility requirements may vary depending on department and batch regulations.

## Additional Coursework

The extra credits required for Honours must usually come from department-approved courses.

These are generally:
- Higher-level electives
- Advanced departmental courses
- Additional project/research work

## BTP Requirement

For Honours students, the B.Tech Project (BTP) is generally mandatory.

The BTP is usually taken during the final year and carries significant credit weight.

## Degree Mention

Students completing all Honours requirements receive an additional Honours mention on their degree.

Example:

```text
B.Tech (Honours)
```

## Important Notes

- Honours requirements are separate from Minor requirements.
- Honours credits usually cannot be double-counted.
- Students should carefully track their additional credits during registration.

Many students begin thinking about Honours after seeing one good CGPA.

Many students stop thinking about Honours after one particularly creative quiz paper.

Both phases are temporary.
