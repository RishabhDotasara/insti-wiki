# Examinations and Grading

Most courses at IIT Madras use a combination of:
- quizzes
- assignments
- mid-semester exams
- end-semester exams

The exact evaluation structure depends on the instructor and is usually announced at the beginning of the semester.

## Typical Evaluation Pattern

A common evaluation structure may include:
- Quiz 1 / Midsem
- Quiz 2
- Assignments or Projects
- Endsem

Typical weightage distribution:
- 20–30% for quizzes/midsems
- 40–60% for endsem

The exact breakup varies from course to course.

Which means every semester begins with:
```text
"This course grading looks manageable."
```

and ends slightly differently.

## Grading System

IIT Madras follows a 10-point grading system.

| Grade | Grade Point |
|---|---|
| S | 10 |
| A | 9 |
| B | 8 |
| C | 7 |
| D | 6 |
| E | 5 |

Passing grades:
```text
S, A, B, C, D, E
```

Fail grade:
```text
U
```

## P Grade (Attendance Grade)

If attendance falls below:

```text
75%
```

students receive a:

```text
P Grade
```

The P grade:
- is separate from academic grading
- acts as an uncompensated fail
- makes students ineligible for supplementary exams in that course

## Supplementary Exams

If a student receives a:

```text
U Grade
```

they may be allowed to write a supplementary exam.

Rules:
- Only one supplementary exam per course
- Missing it requires re-registration of the course

## Contact Courses

Departments may offer:
```text
Contact Courses
```

for final-year students with uncleared core courses or credit shortages.

These are usually crash-course versions intended to clear backlogs.

## Make-Up Exams

Make-up exams may be arranged for:
- medical reasons
- competitions
- institute-related activities

subject to approval.

Approval is generally required from:
- Dean of Academics
- Course Instructor

Make-up exams may apply to:
- quizzes
- midsems
- endsems

depending on instructor permission.

## Re-evaluation and Grades

For issues related to:
- grading
- re-evaluation
- result clarification

students should directly contact the course instructor.

## Important Notes

- Attendance rules are strictly enforced.
- P grades and U grades are treated differently.
- Supplementary exam policies depend on institute rules.

One missed attendance sheet can sometimes have more plot impact than an entire quiz.
