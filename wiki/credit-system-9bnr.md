# Credit System

At IIT Madras, every course contributes a certain number of credits toward graduation. Credits roughly represent the weekly academic workload of a course.

Understanding credits properly is important because:
- Graduation depends on category-wise credit completion
- Honours and minors require additional credits
- Wrong category tracking can create problems during graduation processing

## How Credits Are Calculated

Credits are calculated using the total academic workload of a course.

Typical components include:
- Lecture hours (L)
- Tutorial hours (T)
- Practical/Lab hours (P/O)

![1778331294606-credit_calc.png](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RishabhDotasara/insti-wiki/main/wiki/media/new-article/1778331294606-credit_calc.png)

### Example

```text
L = 3
T = 1
O = 6

Total Credits = 10
```

This means the course contributes 10 credits toward your degree.

## Graduation Credit Requirements

The total number of credits required for graduation depends on:
- Your department
- Your batch
- Whether you pursue Honours or Dual Degree

Typical B.Tech programs require around:

```text
400–430 Credits
```

![1778331333698-credit.png](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RishabhDotasara/insti-wiki/main/wiki/media/new-article/1778331333698-credit.png)

Honours programs generally require additional credits on top of the regular degree requirements.

Always verify your exact curriculum requirements from:
- Academic portal
- Department handbook
- Faculty advisor

> [!IMPORTANT]
> For 2024 and newer batches, several departments have revised their curriculum structures and total credit requirements.  
> The required graduation credits may therefore differ from older batches.

## Course Categories

Courses are divided into different buckets/categories.

These generally include:
- Engineering
- Professional
- Humanities
- Sciences
- Computing
- General
- Management
- Unallotted

Each category has minimum required credits that must be satisfied before graduation.

## Important Workflow Warning

While registering courses in workflow, you may assign categories manually.

However:

> The categories selected during registration are NOT final.

The Academic Section later reviews and reallocates courses into proper buckets during graduation processing.

This creates a dangerous species of problem:

```text
"I thought this counted toward Humanities."
```

It did not.

Always track your category credits independently.

## Semester Credit Limits

There is a maximum limit on how many credits you can register each semester.

### Typical Credit Limits

| Semester | Maximum Credits |
|---|---|
| 1st & 2nd | 56 |
| 3rd & 4th | 66 |
| Later Semesters | CGPA-based |

Higher semester limits depend on your CGPA and Faculty Advisor approval.

## Why Credits Matter

Credits affect:
- Graduation eligibility
- Honours eligibility
- Minor completion
- Dual Degree upgrades
- Internship flexibility
- Placement semester workload

Overloading credits sounds productive until:
- Quiz week arrives
- Three lab reports spawn simultaneously
- Sleep becomes theoretical
