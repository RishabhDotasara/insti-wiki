# Understanding U, I, and P Grades

Apart from regular academic grades, IIT Madras also uses:
- U Grade
- I Grade
- P Grade

These grades have separate academic and administrative implications.

Policies referenced:
- Senate circular on attendance — dated July 26, 2025
- SGAO Eligibility, Ratification & De-ratification Amendment Bill — dated March 10, 2025

## P Grade (Poor Attendance)

The P Grade is an attendance grade and is separate from academic course grading.

### Meaning

A P Grade is given when attendance falls below:

```text
75%
```

It does not affect CGPA directly because it is not treated as an academic grade.

## Important Rules

Students with a P Grade:
- cannot apply for supplementary exams in that course
- receive lower preference during elective allotment
- receive lower preference during IDDD allotment

### Additional Restrictions

Students with more than:

```text
3 P Grades
```

during their tenure are not eligible for:
- YRF
- Honours
- Dual Degree / IDDD / PhD upgradation

Students also cannot claim a Minor if they have a P Grade in a Minor course, except in the final semester.

## PoR and Election Impact

For Academic Year 2025–26:
- Tier 0 and Tier 1 applicants can have at most:
  ```text
  1 P Grade
  ```

From Academic Year 2026–27:
- no P Grades in the previous two semesters are allowed for eligibility
- implementation pending

Students with more than:

```text
2 P Grades
```

are not eligible for elections or ratification for top PoRs.

Attendance suddenly becomes very important the moment someone says:
```text
"Bro you should apply for a core."
```

## U Grade (Fail)

The U Grade indicates failure in a course.

### Meaning

A student receives a U Grade when:
- minimum passing criteria are not met
- the course is not cleared

## Repetition

Students may:
- repeat the course later
- clear the backlog through re-registration

## CGPA Impact

The U Grade itself does not directly affect CGPA for that semester because:
- the course is considered uncleared

## Transcript Clarification

The U Grade does not appear directly on the consolidated transcript.

Instead:
- the transcript shows the number of attempts required to clear the course

Example:
```text
2 Attempts
```

## Placement Impact

Students with backlogs may still apply through placement portals.

However:
- companies may notice multiple attempts during verification

Policies vary company-to-company.

## PoR Impact

Students with active U Grades are ineligible for:
- elections
- Tier 0 PoRs
- Tier 1 PoRs

## I Grade (Incomplete)

The I Grade indicates an incomplete course status.

### Meaning

An I Grade is generally given when:
- quizzes/midsems were completed
- endsem could not be attended
- medical or emergency reasons exist

Skipping endsem generally guarantees:
```text
I Grade
```

## Repetition

Students may repeat the course later to clear the incomplete status.

## CGPA Impact

The course remains incomplete until resolved and does not affect CGPA immediately.

## PoR Impact

Students with any I Grade are ineligible for:
- Tier 0 positions
- Tier 1 positions

as per SGAO guidelines.

## Tier Structure

The IITM PoR structure is divided into tiers.

### Tier 0
Includes:
- Secretaries
- Executive Council
- Hostel positions
- Department Legislators
- Major team heads

### Tier 1
Includes:
- Team Heads
- Major fest heads

### Tier 2 and Below
Includes:
- Coordinators
- Organizers
- General team positions

Ratification for Tier 0 and Tier 1 positions includes strict academic checks.

## Important Notes

- P Grade is attendance-related.
- U Grade is academic failure.
- I Grade is temporary incomplete status.
- All three grades may affect PoRs and academic opportunities differently.

At IITM, grades don’t just affect CGPA anymore.

They occasionally affect your entire student governance career arc too.
