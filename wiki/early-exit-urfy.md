# Early Exit

IIT Madras provides an Early Exit option for students who wish to leave the program before completing the full degree duration.

This option is intended for students who decide that continuing engineering may not align with their interests or future plans.

## Degree Awarded

Students exiting early may receive:

```text
B.Sc in General Engineering
```

or

```text
B.Sc in General Engineering with Specialization
```

depending on the requirements satisfied.

The specialization depends on:
- department criteria
- completed credits
- curriculum requirements

## Eligibility

Students must satisfy:
- minimum credit requirements
- department-specific conditions

before becoming eligible for Early Exit.

The exact specialization requirements are determined individually by departments.

## Important Notes

- Early Exit is generally available during the third year.
- Eligibility depends on completed academic requirements.
- Departments may define different specialization criteria.

The option exists for flexibility.

Sometimes people discover:
- new interests
- different career goals
- startups
- research
- or simply that thermodynamics was not the lifelong soulmate they expected.
