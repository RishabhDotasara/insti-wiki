# Curriculum: Core and Elective Courses

The curriculum at IIT Madras is divided into foundational courses in the early semesters and core/elective courses in the later semesters.

## Years 1–2 (Semesters 1–4): Foundation

During the first two years, students generally take foundational courses such as:
- Mathematics
- Physics
- Chemistry
- Computing
- Engineering Drawing
- Workshop

Along with these, introductory department courses are also included.

The curriculum during these semesters also includes:
- Laboratory courses
- Humanities electives
- Course projects in some semesters

These semesters mainly focus on building fundamentals and introducing students to the academic structure at IITM.

This is also usually the phase where:
- everyone says they’ll “start studying consistently”
- everyone discovers 8 AM classes
- reality begins loading slowly

## Years 3–4 (Semesters 5–8): Core and Electives

The later semesters focus more on:
- Core departmental subjects
- Laboratories
- Elective courses

Students generally take:
- Department electives
- Free electives
- Humanities electives

The final semesters are primarily elective-oriented, and students may also choose to pursue projects instead of some electives.

Compared to the first two years, students generally get significantly more academic flexibility during this phase.

## Internship

Students usually complete a summer internship between the sixth and seventh semesters through the placement and internship process.

## Projects

In the final year, students may choose to do:
- B.Tech Project (BTP)
- Dual Degree Project (DDP)

depending on their program requirements.

Projects may replace some electives depending on the curriculum structure.

## Minors

If the required number of electives are completed from an approved basket, students may earn a Minor in that field.

The exact requirements depend on the approved minor structure.

A surprising number of students discover minors during their pre-placement panic arc.
