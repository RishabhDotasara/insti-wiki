# Miscellaneous Academic Guidelines

This section contains general academic policies, reminders, and important institute procedures that students should keep track of during their degree.

## Dropping Courses

If the academic workload becomes difficult to manage, students may drop courses within approximately:

```text
45 Instructional Days
```

Course dropping is done through the:
- Add/Drop workflow

when announced officially through institute mail.

## Attendance

As per institute policy:

```text
Attendance below 75%
```

results in a:

```text
P Grade
```

for the course.

Attendance policies are strictly enforced.

The phrase:
```text
"They probably won’t check attendance."
```

has historically produced mixed results.

## Academic Calendar

Students are strongly advised to regularly check the Academic Calendar for:
- exam dates
- registration timelines
- holidays
- make-up exam schedules
- semester timelines

Official website:  
[IITM Academic Calendar](https://www.iitm.ac.in/)

## Workflow Compliance

For exceptions such as:
- credit overload
- NOC requests
- special approvals

students may require approval from:
- Faculty Advisor
- HOD
- Dean (Academics)

Academic Section mail:

```text
ugcourses@iitm.ac.in
```

## Semester Drop Due to Medical Reasons

Students may drop a semester due to valid medical reasons through workflow.

Current policy:
```text
Maximum One Semester
```

may be dropped under this provision.

Valid supporting medical documents are required.
