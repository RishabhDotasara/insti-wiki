# Welcome to Campuslores

**Campuslores** is a collaborative knowledge engine built to archive and power our institutional history. It combines the flexibility of hierarchical tagging with a structured moderation workflow, ensuring that student-led documentation is organized, accurate, and accessible to everyone. From academic guides to campus traditions, this is where our collective experience is preserved and shared.
