# NPTEL and Online BS (OB) Credits Transfer

IIT Madras allows students to transfer credits from:
- NPTEL courses
- Online BS (OB) courses

subject to institute and department regulations.

## NPTEL Credit Transfer

Students can transfer up to:

```text
21 IITM Credits
```

from NPTEL online courses.

> Each NPTEL credit equals 3 IITM credits.

These credits:
- do not fall under the semester credit limit of 66
- can count as electives subject to approval
- cannot be used for core courses or advanced DD/M.Tech courses

Up to:

```text
9 Credits
```

from NPTEL can be used toward Humanities requirements.

## Procedure for NPTEL Credit Transfer

1. Enroll in the NPTEL course
2. Complete the course
3. Obtain the certificate
4. Upload the certificate through workflow
5. Seek approval from the HOD

Once approved:
- only the credits are transferred
- grades are not transferred

Official archive:  
[NPTEL Archive](https://archive.nptel.ac.in/)

## Online BS (OB) Credits

Students may also transfer credits from approved Online BS courses.

Unlike NPTEL:
- grades are transferred
- the transferred grades affect CGPA

## Combined Credit Limit

The combined maximum limit for:
- NPTEL credits
- Online BS credits

is:

```text
36 Credits
```

Additional limits:
- Only NPTEL → maximum 21 credits
- Only Online BS → maximum 36 credits

If the limit is exceeded:
- excess credits will not count toward graduation

Replacement of courses within these limits is subject to the discretion of the Dean of Academics.

No reported cases of this so far.  
A mythical side quest, perhaps.

## Dropping an Online BS Course

To drop an OB course:
- A G-form is circulated from ICSR
- The process follows the OB timetable
- Filling the form leads to deregistration

For support:

```text
campus-support@study.iitm.ac.in
```

ICSR office:
- 3rd Floor

## Important Notes

- Approval is mandatory for credit transfer.
- Departments may reject courses that do not satisfy curriculum requirements.
- Credits transferred through NPTEL do not affect CGPA.
- OB grades do affect CGPA.

Before taking a course assuming “it’ll count somewhere,”  
please verify first.
