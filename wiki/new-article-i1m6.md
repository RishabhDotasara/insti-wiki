# Course Registration Process

Course registration at IIT Madras is done online through the academic workflow portal before every semester.

Registration includes:
- Core courses
- Electives
- Credit transfers
- Add/Drop requests
- Minor-related registrations

This is one of the most important academic processes each semester.

And somehow, despite happening every semester, it still manages to feel stressful every single time.

Workflow can generally be accessed:
- using Firefox
- through IITM WiFi

Students outside campus networks may need to use proxy access.

## Registration Procedure

When registration opens:
- A Course Registration tab appears in workflow
- Core courses are already listed
- Students must select them for registration

For electives:
- Use the "View Available Electives" option
- Browse available courses offered that semester
- Add preferred electives

Added electives appear at the bottom of the course list.

Students must assign:
- priorities
- color groups

## Priority System

Priority:
```text
1 = Highest Priority
```

Higher-ranked choices are considered first during allocation.

## Color Grouping

If multiple courses are assigned:
- the same color

then workflow interprets them as:
> "Allocate only one among these."

If different colors are used:
> workflow assumes you want multiple courses.

This system looks simple until you accidentally color-code your entire semester into chaos.

## Course Allocation Methods

Different electives may use different allocation techniques.

### Stratified Random

Students are grouped based on:
- preference ranking position

Students who ranked a course higher are prioritized first.

Within the same group:
- allocation is random

### Random

All applicants are randomly ordered.

Every permutation is equally likely.

Pure chaos. Statistically fair chaos, but still chaos.

### CGPA

Students are ranked based on CGPA.

Higher CGPA students are prioritized first.

Ties are broken randomly.

### COT (Consent of Teacher)

Allocation depends on instructor approval.

Students are generally advised to contact the professor beforehand.

## Credit Limits

### Semester Limits

| Semester | Credit Limit |
|---|---|
| 1st & 2nd | 56 |
| 3rd & 4th | 66 |
| Later Semesters | CGPA-based |

Faculty Advisors have significant control over approval of higher credit loads and advanced courses.

## Finding Electives

The Academic Portal is commonly used for:
- course details
- slot information
- professor details
- classroom details
- allocation techniques

Students are strongly encouraged to:
- check course syllabus
- talk to seniors
- understand grading patterns
- verify workload

before finalizing electives.

Sometimes placeholder names like:
```text
HOD
Course Coordinator
```

appear temporarily instead of professor names.

So yes, talking to seniors is still one of the strongest academic survival techniques at IITM.

## Important Notes

- Add/Drop is generally first come, first serve.
- Faculty Advisor approval is mandatory.
- Seat allocation techniques vary course-to-course.
- Workflow deadlines are strict.

Missing a registration deadline is one of the fastest ways to unlock unnecessary suffering.
